# Security handbook

## Passwords

Passwords must have at least 14 characters and are rotated once a year. A password manager is provided to every employee.

## Devices

Laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after five minutes of inactivity. Lost devices must be reported to the security desk within one hour.

## Incident response

A suspected data breach is reported to the security desk, which opens an incident and informs the data protection officer within 24 hours.
