# Expense policy

## Travel

Book flights at least 14 days ahead in economy class. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 euros per night in Berlin and 150 euros elsewhere.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 40 euros while travelling. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Receipts

Every expense above 25 euros needs a receipt uploaded within 30 days. Claims without receipts are rejected by the finance team.
