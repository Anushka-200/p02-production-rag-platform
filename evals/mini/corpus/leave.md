# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused days up to five may be carried over to March 31.

## Sick leave

An employee who is ill for more than three consecutive days must send a doctor's certificate to the people team. Sick pay is 100 percent for the first 30 days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents receive 8 weeks, which can be split into two blocks within the first year.
